Common questions

Where are my notes stored? Can I get them on another device?

Notes are stored on the device that made them. NDOL has no account and no sync, so notes do not appear on a second device on their own. To move them: Settings → Export all notes writes a text file of everything, and a notebook can be exported as a PDF or as a backup file you can open on another device.

How do I make sure I don't lose my notes?

NDOL keeps three recent automatic backups on the device, and you can restore from any of them in Settings → Restore from backup. That protects against a mistake inside the app — it does not protect against losing the device itself. For that, keep iCloud Backup switched on in iOS Settings, and export anything you can't afford to lose.

I deleted the app and my notes are gone.

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. There is no cloud copy and no way to recover it — not by us either, because we never had it. If you had iCloud Backup on before deleting, restoring the device from that backup may bring it back.

Dictation says it isn't available.

NDOL only transcribes on the device and will not send your voice to a server, so it needs the offline language installed. Go to iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Dictation and make sure Dictation is on and your language is downloaded. On some older devices or less common languages, on-device transcription isn't offered by iOS at all — you can still type notes.

My widget is empty.

Remove the widget and add it again. If it stays empty, open NDOL → Settings and check for an "App Group unavailable" message, which means the app and its widget can't share storage. Email us if you see it.

The Lock Screen / Control Center button.

NDOL adds two controls: one for speaking a note and one for typing. To add one to the Lock Screen, long-press the Lock Screen → Customise → tap the ⊖ on a bottom-corner button → tap + → search NDOL. For Control Center, swipe down from the top-right, long-press an empty area → Add a Control → search NDOL.

Is any of this sent anywhere?

No. NDOL has no network code at all — no account, no server, no analytics, no ads. See the privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Reporting a bug

Email bimnapps@icloud.com with what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. If it involves losing work, say so first — that gets priority.

NDOL

A fast, private note app for iPhone and iPad.

Capture a note by voice or by typing straight from the Lock Screen or Control Center, write and draw in notebooks, and keep reminders, alarms and tasks beside your notes. Nothing you write leaves your device — there is no account, no server and no analytics.

Free to download. One optional in-app purchase, NDOL Premium (US$4.99, one time — not a subscription), unlocks unlimited notebooks, your whole timeline, unlimited alarms, reminders and tasks, every accent colour and the advanced PDF export options. No advertising.

Your notes are stored only on your device

NDOL has no cloud sync and no cloud backup. That has a consequence worth stating plainly:

  • Deleting the app deletes everything in it — permanently, with no way for the developer or anyone else to recover it.
  • If your device is lost, stolen, damaged or reset, the notes on it are gone unless you had a device backup or an export.
  • NDOL keeps up to three automatic backups, but those live on the same device. They protect against a mistake inside the app, not against losing the device.

Export anything you can't afford to lose, and keep iCloud Backup switched on. Settings has a full text export, and notebooks export as PDF or as a restorable backup file. To the extent the law allows, the developer is not liable for lost, corrupted or unrecoverable notes.
